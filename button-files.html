Button Bags Series

Secure bags for organized filing.

What Are COLORS Button Bags?

COLORS button bags are premium plastic document storage solutions designed for professional document organization, protection, and secure storage. These essential office supplies provide reliable storage for documents, papers, and files while maintaining a professional appearance. COLORS button bags are widely used in offices, educational institutions, corporate environments, and professional settings for document management and organization.

COLORS button bags are manufactured from premium polypropylene (PP) and high-quality plastic materials, ensuring durability, clarity, and long-lasting performance. Our collection includes stitched button bags for enhanced durability, envelope bags for classic document storage, double pocket bags for organized storage, and various button bag configurations to meet different organizational needs while maintaining a polished, professional appearance.

About COLORS Button Bags

COLORS button bags offer secure document storage with convenient button closures. Available in stitched button bags, envelope bags, double pocket bags, and printed variants, these bags provide versatile organization for documents and papers. Durable polypropylene construction with reinforced stitching ensures long-lasting performance while protecting contents from dust and moisture. Perfect for offices, schools, and personal document management with custom printing options available for corporate branding.
