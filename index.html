About Us

Our Story

Oswal Gift N Stationery in Sowcarpet, Chennai is known to satisfactorily cater to the demands of its customer base. The business came into existence in 2000 and has, since then, been a known name in its field. We specialize in manufacturing premium report cover files, clip files, clear books, button bags, document bags, sheet protectors, conference folders, zipper bags, card holders, leatherite bags, index separators, paper board files, and display solutions for modern workspaces.

Our Location

We are located at No. 76, Tirupalli Street, Sowcarpet,CHENNAI-79. Our establishment is in close proximity to prominent landmarks, making us easily accessible to our valued customers.

Our Expertise

With over 24 years of experience in the stationery industry, we have established ourselves as leading manufacturers, importers, and distributors of premium plastic files and folders. Our products are crafted using high-quality polypropylene (PP), PVC, and other premium materials that are durable, eco-friendly, and suitable for long-term document storage. We serve customers across India with wholesale and bulk order capabilities, offering customization options including logo printing and custom colors.

Our Commitment

We are committed to delivering exceptional quality, competitive pricing, and outstanding customer service. Our products undergo rigorous quality testing to ensure they meet the highest standards. We continuously innovate our product range to meet the evolving needs of modern offices, educational institutions, and businesses. Our team of experts provides personalized consultation to help customers find the perfect filing and stationery solutions for their specific requirements.
