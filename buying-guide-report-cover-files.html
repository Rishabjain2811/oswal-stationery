Choosing the Right Size

Selecting the correct size is crucial for ensuring your documents fit properly and are adequately protected. COLORS report cover files are available in two primary sizes:

A4 Size

A4 is the standard international paper size (210mm × 297mm) and is the most commonly used size in modern offices. A4 report cover files are ideal for:

  • Standard business documents
  • Reports and presentations
  • Printed materials
  • International business correspondence

FC (Foolscap) Size

FC size (216mm × 330mm) is slightly larger than A4 and is commonly used in India for legal documents, government forms, and traditional business documents. FC report cover files are ideal for:

  • Legal documents
  • Government forms and applications
  • Traditional Indian business documents
  • Documents that require extra space

Pro Tip: Always measure your documents before purchasing report cover files to ensure the perfect fit. Many offices use both A4 and FC sizes, so consider stocking both types.
