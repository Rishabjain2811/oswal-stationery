Paper Board Files Series

Professional document filing solutions.

What Are COLORS Paper Board Files?

COLORS paper board files are premium document organization solutions designed for professional document filing, storage, and presentation. These essential office supplies provide secure storage for documents, papers, and files with durable board construction while maintaining a professional appearance. COLORS paper board files are widely used in offices, educational institutions, corporate environments, and professional settings for document management and organization.

COLORS paper board files are manufactured from premium paper board materials, polypropylene (PP) coatings, and high-quality components, ensuring durability, functionality, and long-lasting performance. Our collection includes lever arch files for secure document holding, ring binders for organized storage, PP printed board lamination for enhanced durability, and various paper board file configurations to meet different organizational needs while maintaining a polished, professional appearance.

About COLORS Paper Board Files

COLORS paper board files provide durable document filing with lever arch mechanisms and ring binders. Featuring double-side PP coating for water resistance and enhanced durability, these files offer professional organization for heavy document storage. Available in lever arch files, ring binders, and PP board variants, they combine sturdy board construction with protective coatings. Ideal for archiving, project documentation, and situations requiring robust document protection with professional presentation.
