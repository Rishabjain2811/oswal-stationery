**Process:**

## Quality Guidelines

### Backlink Quality Criteria
- **Domain Authority**: DA 30+ preferred
- **Relevance**: Must be relevant to office supplies, business, or stationery
- **Traffic**: Target sites with organic traffic
- **Content Quality**: Sites with quality, original content
- **Spam Score**: Low spam score (below 30)

### Red Flags to Avoid
- Link farms and private blog networks
- Irrelevant websites
- Sites with duplicate or thin content
- High spam score websites
- Sites with unnatural link profiles
