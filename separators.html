Index / Separators

Document organization solutions.

What Are COLORS Index Separators?

COLORS index separators are premium document organization solutions designed for professional document sorting, indexing, and categorization. These essential office supplies provide organized document separation and identification while maintaining a professional appearance. COLORS index separators are widely used in offices, educational institutions, corporate environments, and professional settings for document management and organization.

COLORS index separators are manufactured from premium polypropylene (PP), PVC, and high-quality plastic materials, ensuring durability, clarity, and long-lasting performance. Our collection includes document dividers for file organization, file index tabs for quick identification, alphabetical separators for systematic sorting, and various separator configurations to meet different organizational needs while maintaining a polished, professional appearance.

About COLORS Index Separators

COLORS index separators provide systematic document organization with clear tab identification. Available in document dividers, file index tabs, and alphabetical separators, these products enable quick document location within files and binders. Premium construction with reinforced edges ensures durability while maintaining clear visibility of tabs. Ideal for large document collections, reference materials, and situations requiring efficient document categorization and retrieval.
