Choosing the Right Size

Selecting the correct size is crucial for ensuring your documents fit properly and are displayed effectively. COLORS clear books are available in multiple sizes:

A4 Size

A4 is the standard international paper size (210mm × 297mm) and is the most commonly used size in modern offices. A4 clear books are ideal for:

  • Standard business documents
  • Reports and presentations
  • Printed materials and catalogs
  • International business correspondence

FC (Foolscap) Size

FC size (216mm × 330mm) is slightly larger than A4 and is commonly used in India for legal documents, government forms, and traditional business documents. FC clear books are ideal for:

  • Legal documents and contracts
  • Government forms and applications
  • Traditional Indian business documents
  • Documents that require extra space

B4 Size

B4 size (250mm × 353mm) is larger than both A4 and FC, providing more space for documents. B4 clear books are ideal for:

  • Large format documents
  • Certificates and awards
  • Architectural drawings
  • Documents that need prominent display

A3 Size

A3 size (297mm × 420mm) is double the size of A4 and is ideal for larger documents. A3 clear books are perfect for:

  • Large presentations
  • Design portfolios
  • Artwork displays
  • Marketing materials

Pro Tip: Always measure your documents before purchasing clear books to ensure the perfect fit. Many professionals use multiple sizes to accommodate different document types.

Maintenance and Care

Proper maintenance ensures your COLORS clear books last longer and maintain their professional appearance:

Cleaning Tips

  • Wipe with a soft, dry cloth to remove dust
  • For fingerprints, use a slightly damp cloth with mild soap
  • Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners
  • Allow to air dry completely before reuse

Storage Best Practices

  • Keep away from sharp objects

Usage Tips
