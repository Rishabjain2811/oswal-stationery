Sheet Protectors & Document Sleeves

Document protection solutions.

What Are COLORS Sheet Protectors?

COLORS sheet protectors are premium document protection solutions designed for professional document storage, preservation, and display. These essential office supplies provide secure protection for documents, papers, and files while maintaining clarity and accessibility. COLORS sheet protectors are widely used in offices, educational institutions, corporate environments, and professional settings for document management and protection.

COLORS sheet protectors are manufactured from premium polypropylene (PP), PVC, and high-quality plastic materials, ensuring durability, clarity, and long-lasting performance. Our collection includes clear document sleeves for transparent protection, plastic page protectors for page preservation, card protectors for specialized storage, and various sheet protector configurations to meet different protection needs while maintaining a polished, professional appearance.

About COLORS Sheet Protectors

COLORS sheet protectors provide crystal-clear document protection with reinforced edges for durability. Available in clear document sleeves, page protectors, and card protectors, these sleeves offer full visibility while shielding documents from dust, moisture, and scratches. Premium PVC and polypropylene construction ensures long-lasting performance with water-resistant properties. Ideal for important documents, certificates, photos, and frequently referenced materials requiring protection and clear visibility.
